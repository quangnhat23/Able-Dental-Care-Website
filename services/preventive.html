Preventive dentistry

What Is Teeth Cleaning? Teeth cleaning is the process of removing plaque, tartar, and stains above, around, and just below the gumline. They can help prevent cavities, gum disease, and more serious oral health complications.

Scaling and root planing/ Deep Clean is a non-surgical and deep cleaning procedure that removes plaque below the gumline.

What Is Fluoride Treatment? Fluoride treatment involves applying fluoride to the teeth, strengthening tooth enamel and aiding in the prevention of tooth decay.

What and Who got Dental Sealant? We usually put dental sealant for kids under 14. When we apply a dental sealant, we paint a plastic coating on top of your teeth. This makes your teeth smooth and against tooth decay.

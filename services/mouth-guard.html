Mouth guard

Night guards prevent damage caused by bruxism. Bruxism is a disorder that can cause people to unknowingly clench and grind their teeth while asleep. Untreated, it can result in teeth fractures, breakages, headaches, and jaw pain. A custom nightguard fits over your teeth to help protect them from the damage it can cause.
